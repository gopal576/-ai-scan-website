Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Information We Collect

QuickCal AI collects information from you when you register and use the app:

  • Personal Data: Name and email address.
  • Health Metrics: Age, gender, height, and weight to calculate nutritional needs.
  • Scan Data: Images you upload for food analysis.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is primarily used to provide you with nutritional estimates and track your progress.

  • AI Analysis: We use the Gemini AI API (by Google) to identify food items in your images.
  • Nutrition Calculation: Automated calculations based on your physical metrics.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with key infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase: Our database and authentication provider.
  • Gemini AI: For food recognition (images only).

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information stored in our database.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your account and personal data at any time through the app settings or by contacting support.
